#  Favorite Movies

## 1. *Avatar: The Way of Water*

> “The way of water connects all things. The sea is our home before our birth and after we die.”

###  Overview
*Avatar: The Way of Water* (2022) continues Jake Sully’s journey on **Pandora**, where he and Neytiri protect their family from returning human invaders.  
The story dives into oceanic tribes and explores themes of *family, survival,* and *connection to nature.*

---

###  Key Themes
1. Environmental preservation  
2. Family unity  
3. Respect for culture and nature  

###  Main Characters
- **Jake Sully** – Former human, now Na’vi leader.  
- **Neytiri** – Strong and protective mother.  
- **Kiri** – Adopted daughter with a mysterious link to Eywa.  
- **Lo’ak** – Jake’s brave but reckless son.  

---

## 2. *The Greatest Showman*

> “No one ever made a difference by being like everyone else.”

###  Overview
*The Greatest Showman* (2017) tells the inspiring story of **P.T. Barnum**, who rises from nothing to create a spectacular circus celebrating diversity and imagination.

---

###  Key Themes
1. Dreaming big  
2. Embracing uniqueness  
3. Overcoming prejudice  

###  Main Characters
- **P.T. Barnum** – Visionary showman.  
- **Phillip Carlyle** – Partner and performer.  
- **Anne Wheeler** – Trapeze artist who faces social barriers.  
- **Charity Barnum** – Barnum’s supportive wife.  

---

###  Quick Facts
| Detail | Information |
|:--|:--|
| **Director** | Michael Gracey |
| **Release Year** | 2017 |
| **Genre** | Musical, Drama |
| **Runtime** | 105 minutes |



---

###  Fun Facts
- Both films celebrate **connection** — with people or nature.  
- *Avatar: The Way of Water* uses groundbreaking **underwater motion capture**.  
- *The Greatest Showman* became a **global musical phenomenon**.
